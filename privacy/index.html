Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Contact: ak1694013@gmail.com

Wolfhollow is an online Werewolf game for phones. It is designed to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking. This policy explains what the app processes, why, and for how long.

What we process

DataWhyHow long
Nickname and avatar you chooseShown to the other players in your gameOnly while your game is running. Kept in the game server's memory, never written to disk
Random app ID created on your deviceLets you rejoin your game after losing signal. A one-way scrambled version of it lets other players block you without learning the IDOn your device until you uninstall the app; in server memory while you play
Chat messagesDelivered to the other players in your gameOnly while your game is running
Reports you send, or that others send about you (the reason, nicknames, scrambled IDs, room code and the reported player's recent messages)To enforce our community rules and keep games safeDeleted automatically after 90 days
Anonymous usage events such as “game started”, “game finished” or which screen was opened, with app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate countryTo understand which features people use and to fix problems. Never includes your nickname, chat messages or room codesUp to 12 months, then deleted
Connection data (IP address, app version)To connect you to the game server and protect it from abuseThe game itself does not store IP addresses. Our hosting provider may keep standard connection logs for a short time

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers. Your settings (nickname, avatar, blocked players and preferences) are stored only on your device.

Usage analytics and your choice

Anonymous usage events are sent to PostHog, our analytics provider, which stores them in the United States. IP addresses are discarded before events are stored; only an approximate country is kept. You can turn this off at any time in the app under Settings → Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, nothing is sent.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. Two service providers process data on our behalf: our cloud hosting provider, which runs the game server, and PostHog, which stores the anonymous usage events. Reports may also be forwarded to a private moderation channel that only our moderators can see.

Children

Wolfhollow is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us a report or other data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because Wolfhollow has no accounts and does not keep game history, there is usually nothing stored about you once your game ends. To ask about, or delete, report data involving you, or to exercise any other privacy right that applies where you live, email ak1694013@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Security

Connections between the app and the game server are encrypted. Game data lives only in memory and disappears when a game ends.
