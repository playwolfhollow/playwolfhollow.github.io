Common questions

How do I join a friend's game?

Ask them for the 5-letter room code, or tap the invite link they sent. In Wolfhollow, tap Join with a code and enter it.

The app is stuck on “Connecting…”

Check that your phone is online, then close and reopen the app. If you were in a game, tap Return to game on the home screen to get back in.

Someone is rude or cheating. What can I do?

Tap their name during the game and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides their messages straight away. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Wolfhollow has no accounts, and games aren't saved after they end. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. To ask about a report involving you, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

In the app, open Settings and turn off Share anonymous usage data.

Is Wolfhollow free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
